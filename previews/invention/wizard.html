When you see a problem that needs solving, what do you do first?

Builder
I immediately start thinking about how to make the thing. I want to know what it looks like and how it works.
Empath
I think about the people affected first. I want to understand how they feel before I think about solutions.
Systems Thinker
I ask why the problem exists in the first place. I want to understand what broke before I fix it.
Connector
I look for what already exists that could be combined or repurposed. I find solutions hiding in other fields.
Storyteller
I think about how to make people care. I want to find the words or images that make the idea feel real.
