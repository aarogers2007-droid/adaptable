Your invention

AquaPure Gravity Filter

A modular water purification system that uses gravity and locally sourced bio-materials to deliver clean drinking water to off-grid communities without electricity.

The problem

780 million people worldwide lack access to clean drinking water, and most purification systems require electricity or expensive replacement filters that rural communities cannot maintain.

How it works

The filter uses a three-stage gravity-fed system: a ceramic pre-filter made from local clay, a biochar layer created from agricultural waste, and a UV-transparent collection chamber that uses sunlight for final disinfection. Each stage can be rebuilt using materials found within 10 miles of any tropical or subtropical village.

Who it helps

Families in rural communities across Sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, and Central America who currently walk over a mile to access water that is often contaminated.

Why it matters

Clean water access is the single highest-leverage intervention for child health outcomes — this invention makes it sustainable without dependency on imported parts or outside maintenance.
