1. What Is This Platform?

Your organization now has a personalized AI learning platform, fully branded as yours. Students visit your website, see your logo and colors, and go through a curriculum guided by an AI mentor that adapts to each student individually.

How It Works

Your organization uploads its own curriculum materials, and the platform transforms them into interactive, personalized lessons. Each lesson is a conversation with an AI mentor that knows the student's interests, their business idea, and where they are in the program. The AI teaches by asking questions, not by lecturing. Students build a real business concept from scratch.

3. What Students Experience

Students never see behind the curtain. They see your brand, your logo, your colors. The experience feels like your organization's product.

The Student Journey

Step 1: Discovery (Ikigai Wizard)

Students answer questions about what they love, what they're good at, what the world needs, and how they could get paid. The AI synthesizes these into a personalized business idea. This takes about 10 minutes.

Data produced: Business idea (niche, name, target customer, revenue model) for every student. Searchable and exportable.

Step 2: Learning (AI-Guided Lessons)

Each lesson is a one-on-one conversation with an AI mentor. The mentor asks questions, builds on the student's answers, and guides them through concepts like finding customers, setting prices, building a brand, and launching. The AI adapts its language and complexity to the student's grade level.

Data produced: Per-lesson completion status, time spent, number of AI exchanges, checkpoint mastery. All tracked per student and in aggregate.

Step 3: Simulation (Scenario Challenges)

Students role-play real business situations: handling a difficult customer, running a pop-up shop, managing a budget crisis. The AI plays different characters and evaluates the student's decisions against a rubric.

Data produced: Scenario completion rates, badge levels earned (bronze/silver/gold), criteria met per scenario.

Step 4: Identity (Archetype Card)

After completing the initial discovery, each student receives an AI-generated "thinker profile" card that describes who they are as a builder. Students can download it as a PDF or share it via a unique link.

Data produced: Archetype distribution across your student population. How many Architects vs. Catalysts vs. Navigators.

4. Getting Started (Step by Step)

For You (Program Administrator)

  1. Sign in at your program URL. Use Google sign-in or the email and password you set up during onboarding.
  2. Review your dashboard. You'll see an overview of your program with zero students. This is normal. The charts will populate as students join.
  3. Copy your invite code. Your dashboard shows a code (e.g., "VLAB26"). This is what students use to join. You can also copy a direct link.
  4. Share the invite code through your channels: email lists, program announcements, events, social media. Students go to your program URL and enter the code.
  5. Watch the data come in. As students complete lessons, your dashboard updates in real time. You'll see completion rates, engagement metrics, and alerts.

For Students

  1. Visit the program URL (e.g., [your-program-url]/go).
  2. Enter the invite code provided by your organization.
  3. Create an account using Google or email.
  4. Complete the discovery wizard (about 10 minutes). The AI asks about their interests and generates a business idea.
  5. Start lessons. Students work through lessons at their own pace. Each lesson is a conversation that takes 10-20 minutes.
What you see: As each student joins and progresses, your dashboard shows their status in real time. You don't need to check in with individual students unless the system flags one for you.

Alerts Tab

The system automatically detects three things:

  • Crisis signals: A student expressed hopelessness or distress. Flagged as urgent.
  • Inactive students: A student hasn't been active in 3+ days.

7. Student Safety

The platform serves minors. Safety is not a feature, it is the foundation.

What the Platform Does Automatically

ProtectionHow It Works
Crisis DetectionEvery student message is scanned for signs of self-harm, suicidal ideation, abuse, or severe distress. Works in any language. If detected, an urgent alert is sent to your admin dashboard immediately.
Content ModerationStudent inputs are scanned for inappropriate content before reaching the AI. Flagged content is blocked and an alert is created.
AI Output FilteringEvery word the AI sends to a student is filtered for profanity (in 10 languages), age-inappropriate content, personal information requests, and hallucinated URLs. Filtered content never reaches the student.
Age AdaptationThe AI adapts its language, examples, and business scope based on the student's grade level. A 7-year-old gets lemonade-stand ideas. A 16-year-old gets e-commerce concepts.
Data EncryptionAll student data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Student data is isolated to your organization and never shared with other organizations.

What You Need to Do

  • Check your Alerts tab regularly. Crisis alerts require human follow-up. The AI pauses the lesson and suggests the student talk to a trusted adult, but a real person needs to close the loop.
  • Have a point person. Designate someone on your team who monitors alerts and responds within 24 hours.
Why this matters for sponsors: You can tell funders: "Our platform includes automatic crisis detection in every language, content moderation on all AI outputs, and age-appropriate adaptation for every grade level." That's a sentence most edtech companies cannot say.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take a student to complete the program?
Completion time depends on your curriculum. Each lesson is a 10-20 minute conversation. Students work at their own pace and can pause and resume anytime.
Can students use this on Chromebooks?
Yes. The platform is built for school Chromebooks. It works in any modern web browser with no downloads, no apps, and no special software. No microphone or camera required.
Is student data private?
Yes. Student data is encrypted, isolated to your organization, and never shared with other organizations or third parties. You can request full data deletion at any time.
What if a student says something concerning?
The platform automatically detects crisis language in any language and creates an urgent alert on your dashboard. The AI pauses the lesson and encourages the student to talk to a trusted adult. Crisis hotline information is provided to the student automatically.
Can the AI give students bad advice?
The AI teaches by asking questions, not by giving advice. It guides students to think through their own business decisions. Every AI response is filtered for age-inappropriate content, profanity, and personal information requests before the student sees it. The AI is also tested: we simulate 198 lesson conversations across 9 different student types before any update ships.
Can I customize the curriculum?
Your organization uploads its own curriculum materials. Our AI transforms them into interactive, personalized lessons. The number of lessons depends on your curriculum. You can upload PDFs, documents, and videos that the AI will reference during lessons.
How do I share the data with sponsors?
Click "Export PDF" on your dashboard to generate a branded Impact Report. It includes your logo, key stats, and charts. You can also export raw data as a CSV spreadsheet for custom analysis.
What if my students speak different languages?
The AI mentor adapts to the student's language register. Crisis detection works in any language. The interface is currently in English. Full multilingual support is on our roadmap.
What does it cost?
Volume pricing starts at $14.99/student/year. Contact us for custom quotes.
Do students see your company name?
No. Students see your organization's name, logo, and colors. The platform is fully branded to your organization. Students experience it as your product.
