2. The Reveal — Student's Final Card (3 example titles)

THE

Frontier

A quiet observer who notices the small things that everyone else walks past, and wonders what would happen if those things were different.

THE WISHYou want to hold something real in your hands when the work is done.
THE MINDYour first instinct when something is broken is to figure out how to fix it yourself.
THE LENSYou carry knowledge about the natural world that most people your age never think about.
THE SCALEYou measure success by whether the whole world shifted, even slightly.
THE VOICEYou reach for a pencil before you reach for words.

THE

Hearth

Someone who feels the temperature of a room before anyone speaks, and quietly makes sure everyone in it has what they need.

THE WISHYou imagine a place where people can gather and feel safe, even if it only exists in your mind right now.
THE MINDWhen you see someone struggling, your first thought is about what they must be feeling, not what went wrong.
THE LENSYou understand how people from different backgrounds experience the same moment in completely different ways.
THE SCALEYou care most about the ten people you can see, not the ten thousand you cannot.
THE VOICEYou explain things by telling a story about someone you know.

THE

Legend

A person who believes the right words at the right time can change what an entire generation thinks is possible.

THE WISHYou see something no one has named yet and feel a pull to give it language.
THE MINDBefore you solve a problem, you need to find the image or phrase that makes other people care about it too.
THE LENSYou know things about music and rhythm that let you hear patterns most people walk right past.
THE SCALEYou think in terms of millions, not because you want fame, but because the idea deserves it.
THE VOICEYou make a video before you write a document, because you need to hear the idea spoken to know if it is real.
