Vision
What I Love
Drawing, painting, pet portraits, watercolors
What I'm Good At
Capturing likeness, color mixing, photo references
What People Need
Affordable custom pet portraits, personal gifts
How I Get Paid
Per-portrait pricing, $15-35 by size
What We Do
Custom watercolor pet portraits for classmates and their families — hand-painted from photos, delivered framed or unframed
Why This Matters to Me
I've been drawing animals since I was 8 and people always say my portraits "look alive." Every portrait I paint is a gift someone gives to a person who loves their pet.
The Golden Circle
WHY: Every pet owner wants to freeze a moment with their best friend. HOW: Hand-painted from the owner's favorite photo. WHAT: Custom watercolor pet portraits.

Customer
Target Customer
Girls at school whose families have pets — especially around Christmas, Mother's Day, and pet birthdays. The buyer is usually a parent paying for a gift, or a student buying for their own room.
Customer Profile
Sarah, 15. Has a golden retriever named Biscuit that she posts on Instagram constantly. Her mom's birthday is next month and she wants something personal but can't afford a $200 Etsy commission.
Revenue Model
$15 for a 5×7 pencil sketch, $25 for an 8×10 watercolor, $35 for 11×14 with background detail. Payment via Venmo or cash. Takes 3-5 days per portrait.
Interview Insights
4 interviews completed
People don't just want a picture of their pet — they want the PERSONALITY captured. "Make him look goofy, that's how he really is."

Competition & Differentiation
Research Your Competition
My main competitors are Etsy portrait artists ($60-200, 2-4 week wait) and phone filter apps (free but generic). My edge: I'm local, fast (3-5 days), cheap ($15-35), and I know the pets personally.
Define Your Target Customer
I'm not competing with professional artists. I'm competing with the "I'll just print a photo at Walgreens" default. My customer wants something more personal than a photo but can't justify $100+.

Marketing & Sales
Zero-Budget Marketing
Three channels: Instagram process videos, school art hallway with QR code, word of mouth. First action: DM 5 classmates with pet Instagrams this week.
Social Media
1-1-1 rule: one process timelapse, one customer reaction, one behind-the-scenes. Post 3x/week.
Word of Mouth
Remarkable moment: tiny bonus sketch on the back. Referral script: "I painted Sarah's dog — want me to do yours?" 5 people to tell this week.

The Numbers
Cost Per Portrait (8×10 watercolor)
Paper: $2 · Paints: $1 · Frame: $4 · Time: ~2 hours
Total cost: $7 → At $25 price = $18 profit
Pricing Rationale
Etsy portraits start at $60. Framing alone costs $15. At $25, I'm the only option between "free phone photo" and "$60+ art."
Tracking System
Google Sheet: Date | In | Out | What For
Weekly review: Sundays 7pm
Two questions: Am I making more than I spend? Biggest cost I could cut?

Launch & First Sale
MVP
One 5×7 pencil sketch of Biscuit. Not watercolor, not framed. Perfectionism traps I'm ignoring: "I need a website," "I should practice more breeds." Delivering Thursday.
First Customer Protocol
Confirmation DM within 1 hour. Risk plan: likeness off → show WIP; doesn't like it → free redo. Remarkable moment: bonus sunglasses sketch on the back.
Feedback
Two questions: "Walk me through showing it to your mom" + "One thing you'd change?" Pre-committed: if "too slow" → offer $5 rush option.
After the Sale
Post the portrait on Instagram. DM three people who liked it. Next targets: Diego (cat), Mrs. Chen (puppy), art teacher (2 rescues). Wisdom: "The hardest part was the first DM."
